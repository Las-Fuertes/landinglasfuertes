## Texto para pegar (inglés)

**Business.** Fundación Las Fuertes is a Colombian non-profit born on Isla Fuerte (Bolívar) and run
by a team that grows from Bogotá. It delivers Comprehensive Menstrual Education (EMI) to girls and
adolescents in 7 territories of Colombia: Isla Fuerte, Córdoba, Bolívar, Atlántico, La Guajira,
Ibagué and Bogotá D.C. It is funded by donations of money, goods and time.

**Website role.** The website (lasfuertes.org, in Spanish, English and French) is not used by the
beneficiaries. Its users are supporters: individual donors in Colombia and abroad, remote
volunteers and partners. Its single job is to turn visitors into supporters through the "Súmate"
(join us) panel.

**Programs (the "educational map").**

- EMI workshops, ages 10 to 14
- Reading and fun clubs, ages 6 to 11
- "Mi ruta, mi destino" (self-esteem and life project), ages 12 to 14
- "Chiquifuertes" (body awareness through play and drawing), ages 6 to 10
- "Voces soberanas": youth leadership and community work, ages 14+

**Program results shown on the site (offline metrics, not measured by the website).** About 550
girls, adolescents and women in workshops; understanding of menstruation as a natural cycle rose
from 45% to 95%; more than 50 eco menstrual products delivered (at least 6 kg of waste avoided per
user per year); 8 out of 10 participants say they know themselves better.

**Ways to support and payment rails.**

- One-time donation in COP via Bold (card, international cards, PSE), minimum COP 5,000
- Monthly donation via a Mercado Pago subscription
- Direct transfer: Nequi, Daviplata, Bancolombia
- From the U.S.: tax-deductible donations in USD through partner Caring for Colombia (501(c)(3)) on
  Give Lively, one-time or monthly
- In-kind: used laptops, cameras, school and art supplies, children's books in Spanish
- Clothing for the Llegue-Llegue, the foundation's second-hand clothing shop on the island
- Remote volunteering: graphic design and social media, lawyers and accountants, fundraising and
  partnerships, educational materials
- Spreading the word on Instagram (@las.fuertes) and LinkedIn

**Website North Star.** Completed donations (count and amount), split by one-time vs monthly.

**Website journey.** Landing → child-protection notice accepted → story sections (intro, welcome,
EMI, donations, educational map, impact, who we are) → open "Súmate" (from the donations button,
floating button, footer or a shared link) → choose how to help (money, goods, time) → one-time or
monthly → amount → payment opened → thank-you page (approved, pending or rejected).

**Input metrics.** Visit to "Súmate" open rate; open to donation-started rate; payment approval
rate; share of monthly vs one-time; volunteer and in-kind WhatsApp clicks; traffic by language and
country.

**Privacy constraints.** The site shows photos of minors behind a protection notice. Session
replays must never show images; no personal data is collected on the site (payments happen on Bold
and Mercado Pago).
